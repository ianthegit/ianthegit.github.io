How to Play Gridle

Gridle is a dynamic intersecting word puzzle centered around a 3x3 core box!

E
The center letter is fixed in place as an anchor.
W
Valid Words light up in glowing golden-emerald when correctly formed!

Rules & Gameplay:

  • Every horizontal row and vertical column in the grid MUST form a valid Oxford English dictionary word (4 to 8 letters).
  • Plurals and common words are allowed; proper nouns are excluded.
  • Drag and drop tiles from the tray or tap to select & place tiles.
  • Complete all intersecting words to win!
