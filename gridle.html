How to Play Gridle

Welcome to Gridle: Overhanging Frame Edition! Solve the 4-word frame where words intersect and overhang past corners.

The 4 Corner Anchors are locked in place as given anchor letters!
W
Valid Words light up with glowing golden-emerald ribbons when complete!

Overhanging Mechanics & Rules:

  • The 4 words (Top, Bottom, Left, Right) form a frame, but words can overhang past the intersection points! The anchors do not need to be at the start or end of the words.
  • Drag tiles from the tray to grid slots, between grid slots, or drag placed tiles back to the tray to return them.
  • Tap a tile to select and place it, or tap a placed tile on the grid to instantly return it to your tray.
  • Use the Undo button to rewind your moves step-by-step.
  • Vowel tiles in the tray are subtly tinted with soft indigo styling.
  • Complete all 4 words simultaneously with valid Oxford dictionary words to win!
