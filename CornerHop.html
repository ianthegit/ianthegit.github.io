How to Play CornerHop

  • Goal: Drag or tap tiles from the bottom tray to form 4 valid intersecting words (or BODMAS equations) along the 5x5 outer frame.
  • Corners: Corner tiles intersect both horizontal and vertical lines.
  • MathsHop: Equations use numbers 1-99 and strictly follow BODMAS/PEMDAS.
  • ⚡ Eddie Mode: Enabled by default! Only the top-left tile is revealed at start. Turning it OFF locks it off for that attempt.
  • Daily Lock: Solved puzzles are locked per date and mode. Refreshing will bring up your completed daily result!
