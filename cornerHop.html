How to Play CornerHop

Objective: Complete the 4-word overhanging frame by placing tiles into empty spaces.

1

Fixed Anchors: Dark green corner tiles are locked fixed letters forming the intersections.

2

Overhanging Frame: Words stretch horizontally across Top & Bottom, and vertically down Left & Right sides.

3

Drag or Tap: Touch or drag tiles from the tray directly into empty grid positions, or tap a tile then tap a cell.

Tip: All 4 frame words must be legitimate English dictionary words simultaneously!
