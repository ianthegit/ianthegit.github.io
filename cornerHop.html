How to Play

Objective: Complete all 4 overhanging frame edges with valid words (CornerHop) or arithmetic equations (MathsHop).

1

Corner Intersections: Standard mode pre-locks corner anchors. Eddie Mode hides all anchors except top-left for an extreme challenge! Once toggled off, it locks off for that session.

2

Maths Mode (BODMAS): Pre-placed '=' sign anchors divide the equation. Operations follow strict order of operations (* and / before + and -).

3

Drag or Tap: Drag tiles directly from the bottom tray into empty slots or tap a tile and tap a slot to place it.

Hints: You get a maximum of 3 hints per puzzle. Hinted tiles are marked yellow on your final share grid.
